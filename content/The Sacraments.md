>*"The spiritual virtue of a sacrament is like light; although it passes among the impure, it is not polluted."*                                                                                                                      -St. Augustine

![[Sacraments.png]]

In the [[The Catholic Church]], as sacrament is defined as:
>*The The sacraments are efficacious signs of grace, instituted by Christ and entrusted to the Church, by which divine life is dispensed to us.”*                                           -CCC 1131

In practice, this means that a sacrament is a material means for believers of the christian faith to engage with God. There are seven sacraments, however it is important to distinguish between something being a sacramental vs. being a sacrament. To be "sacramental" is to be holy things instituted by [[The Catholic Church|the church]] and not by Christ (an example of something sacramental would be [[The Rosary]]).

The seven sacraments of the church are:

1. [[Baptism]]
2. [[Eucharist]]
3. [[Confirmation]]
4. [[Reconciliation]]
5. [[Healing of the Sick]]
6. [[Matrimony]]
7. [[Holy Orders]]
