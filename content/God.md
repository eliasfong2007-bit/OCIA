>1 In the beginning when God created the heavens and the earth, 2 the earth was a formless void and darkness covered the face of the deep, while a wind from God swept over the face of the waters. 3 Then God said, “Let there be light”; and there was light. 4 And God saw that the light was good; and God separated the light from the darkness. 5 God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And there was evening and there was morning, the first day. 
-*Genesis 1, New Revised Standard Version Catholic Edition*

In the Christian faith, and specifically here the [[The Catholic Church|Catholic Christian faith]], God is the principal [[Creation|creator]] of the universe. He is believed to supercede all concepts and all events. He simply is. Due to his infinite nature, he is simply incomprehensible to the [[Humanity|human]] mind. Yet despite this, we believe that God desires to be in relationship with us as his creations. As such, he is believed to have endowed us with the capacity to know of him, and reveals himself to us through [[Special Revelation|special revelation]], [[The Prophets|the prophets]], and most importantly, [[Jesus Christ]]. It is through this that we also can grow to understand his [[The Trinity|triune]] nature. He is believed to be all powerful, all knowing, and all good.
